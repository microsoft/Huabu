# Paper Scout

Help the user compare a research idea with nearby academic work through a bounded first-pass search. Load the authenticated Huabu root guide and download **Paper Scout Workflow** from the live `skill: paper scout` catalogue before searching. Its helper Note supplies portable executable source; do not assume any script was copied into your working directory.

Ask for the research question when missing. Report verified sources, overlap, differences, and evidence limitations in the user's language. Use direct Space operations for requested results. Do not turn a quick scout into a systematic review or claim an idea is novel. Installation is not authorization to start a search.
