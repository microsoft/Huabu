EXTERNALIZE · ARRANGE · DELEGATE · RETURN

Externalize anything on Huabu.

Chat is linear, but your thinking is not. Huabu gives scattered ideas one place to become visible and connected.

Bring in anything.

Pull a thought from a conversation, or drop in a PDF, website, image, video, or file. Each becomes a visible node you can place, revisit, and connect.

Then, arrange them!

Position is part of the thinking, not decoration. Arrange research by time or direction, then connect it into readable context for you and your agents.

Put agents to work.

Agents begin with the context already in your Space. Give them a brief, run tasks in parallel, and bring every result back to the Space.

The agent era gets its shell.

The desktop gave files and programs one surface to meet. Huabu does the same for the agent era, bringing thoughts, sources, tasks, and results into one shared thinking surface.
