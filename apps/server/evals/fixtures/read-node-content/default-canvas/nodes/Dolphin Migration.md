# Dolphin Migration

Pacific bottlenose dolphins follow the warm Kuroshio current along the
eastern coast each spring. Pods of 30–60 individuals have been tagged
near Cape Erimo and tracked as far north as the Sea of Okhotsk.

The single most distinctive fact about this corridor is the **3047 km
average annual round trip** that adult females complete between calving
grounds in southern Honshu and the summer feeding banks off Hokkaido.

Conservation note: shipping-lane density along the Tsugaru Strait has
tripled since 2018, and acoustic-disturbance studies attribute the
**42% decline** in echolocation clicks per dive to vessel noise.
