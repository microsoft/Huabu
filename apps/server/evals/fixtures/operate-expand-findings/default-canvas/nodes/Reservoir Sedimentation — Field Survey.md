# Reservoir Sedimentation — Field Survey

The 2025 Longmen reservoir survey combined single-beam bathymetry with
core sampling at 18 stations. Four findings stand out and each is
self-contained enough to become its own node.

## Finding 1 — Delta progradation

The upstream delta has advanced **310 m** toward the dam since the 2019
survey, concentrating coarse sediment in the backwater reach rather than
spreading it across the pool.

## Finding 2 — Capacity loss

Live storage has dropped **7.4%** in six years — roughly double the
design-life projection — driven almost entirely by the delta in Finding 1.

## Finding 3 — Turbidity-current bypass

Two monitored flood events produced turbidity currents that carried fine
sediment **past the intake tower** and out the low-level outlet, a natural
bypass worth exploiting operationally.

## Finding 4 — Dredging priority zone

Combining the above, the highest-value dredging zone is the **1.2 km
backwater reach**, not the near-dam pool where dredging is usually focused.
